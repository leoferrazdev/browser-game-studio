# Poki

## Operational summary

- Treat early access, review, submission, and publication as separate states.
- Keep the Poki adapter and SDK behavior isolated from the base game.
- Prepare truthful project, developer, game, and technical information.
- Revalidate the current early-access and submission requirements before transmission.

## Source

- Developer share form: https://developers.poki.com/share

The application process and requirements are volatile; do not infer approval from a completed form.
